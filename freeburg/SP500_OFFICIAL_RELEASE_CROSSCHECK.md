Those public announcements match the research archive **to approximately
two decimal places**. The original workbook records up to six decimal
places, so these releases do **not** establish exact numeric equivalence,
nor do they establish the first release time of revisions.

S&P press releases do not resolve the *as-reported quarterly EPS* inputs
required to reproduce Pu Shen. Therefore it would be misleading to say
that all missing quarters have been independently reacquired.

## Source hierarchy and restrictions

Use only (1) original dated S&P workbooks (direct or archived) or (2) dated,
first-party S&P quarterly publications as alternative source evidence.
Retain the original report URLs and timestamps. Do **not** substitute
untraceable third-party GitHub datasets for the primary evidence.

The four known inaccessible snapshot dates remain 2023-06-09,
2024-05-07, 2025-05-28, 2026-01-28. The earliest 17 retrieved official
archived snapshots cover 138 quarters exactly, as verified previously.

The independent acquisition problem is now **partially resolved for the
missing dividends at rounded precision** but **not resolved for exact
reported EPS and full numerical reproducibility**.
